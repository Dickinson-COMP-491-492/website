# RD06 - Hadley Wickham Roundtable Discussion

## Preparation

Review the expectations for preparation for the [Readings and Class Discussions](./RD-ReadingsAndDiscussion.md).

## Assigned Readings

1. Read [Hadley Wickham](https://hadley.nz/)'s Personal Bio page and explore the links.
2. [Hadley Wickham, the Man Who Revolutionized R](https://priceonomics.com/hadley-wickham-the-man-who-revolutionized-r/).
3. [For the Love of Statistics](https://blog.coursera.org/love-statistics-conversation-hadley-wickham/).
4. Read some of [Hadley Wickham's Substack Posts](https://substack.hadley.nz/archive) that interest you.
5. Watch these few short videos:
   - A [Thank You to the RStudio/Posit Open Source community](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vvxwSc3ShQA)(1:35).
   - [Foundations by Hadley Wickham](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoOBZuWxKug)(6:36).

## Discussion Questions

1. Prepare at least 3 questions that you would like to ask Hadley during our discussion.
   - You are unlikely to be able to ask all of them, but having a variety will make it more likley that one will fit naturally into the conversation.
